    Typical workflow

    1. Open a .glb (or drag one in, or pick one from the Library), or click Dummy.
    2. Bone mode → Stick figure, or Draw bones and click joints into the model. Drag joints to fit.
    3. Bind attaches the model's surface to the skeleton.
    4. Switch to Animate, move along the timeline and turn joints — keys are saved for you.
    5. Close loop, turn on Loop, press play. Then Save.

    Fixing a bad download

    • Vertex/Edge mode: pick points or lines and drag them.
    • Attach snaps picked points together; Detach pulls a point apart so the faces around it can separate.
    • Merge by distance welds points that are almost on top of each other (closes cracks).
